# Attribution

The collection — which 21 laws belong together, and the framing of them as *laws of
UX* — is Jon Yablonski's [1]. This library credits that curation and then goes back to
each law's primary source, cited below. The reference files synthesise the findings in
our own words and extend them to non-visual surfaces; none reproduce Yablonski's prose.
